# Changelog

Player-facing changes for each release of Terrestrial Tribulations. The release zip's version comes from `pack_version` in `modpack/curseforge-mods.json`; bump both together.

## 0.9.0 (first public beta, not released yet)

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.18, 163 mods. Recommended memory: 8 GB.

### Story
- **The main campaign, Chapters 1–12**, in the quest book: from the Hollow fields to founding Emberhold, the Shattered Realms, the Iron Choir and the fight for the Crown. It has three endings.
- **Nine story NPCs** with their own skins and branching conversations: Elder Maera, Doran, Captain Rhosk, Pell, Tamsin, Ilsabet, the Stranger, Wren and Archivist Sol. Later chapters' talks unlock as you progress, and finishing a talk ticks off its quest.
- **Every story NPC lives somewhere:** Elder Maera and Doran wait at a refugee camp beside spawn, and the others have their own outposts, ruins, groves and towers out in the world. Each site stays closed until its chapter, and the quest that sends you there gives you a map. Later, most of them will move into Emberhold when you invite them, settling inside the matching building (Doran at the Blacksmith's anvil, Sol at the Library's lectern, and so on), at a different spot each time you come back. Story NPCs carry their story weapons and tools (Rhosk his Thornwatch Halberd, Pell his Cogwright Breaker, Ilsabet her Prism Ward Staff...), strike fitting poses and work while you're nearby.
- **Shops** run by Doran, Pell, Ilsabet and the Stranger, priced for the chapter you meet them in.
- **26 existing bosses take story roles**, from the Twilight wardens to Malric, the Hollow Regent. Each one's story name shows on its boss bar, in death messages and in the quest book, and most speak lines when you fight them.
- Story gates: the realm dimensions (Aether, Twilight Forest, Undergarden, Blue Skies, Otherside) open in Chapter 7, and The End, the last of the Convergence, opens in Chapter 10. Hero suits and powers arrive with their chapters. A chat line tells you the moment each one opens.

### Side Paths (optional quest tabs)
- **Cogwright Workshop** (Create), **The Deep Engines** (Mekanism and AE2), **Lumen Conclave Studies** (Iron's Spells, Ars Nouveau, Apotheosis), **Emberhold Builders** (MineColonies) and **Wayfarer's Guide** (exploring and tools). Each tab opens when the story introduces its faction.

### Combat and character
- **23 custom weapons**, each with its own 3D model, colours, swing trail and ability. They include karambits, longswords, a halberd, gauntlets, bows, a staff and more.
- Weapon skills with real effects: Ashfang's Ember Hook pulls you behind your target, the Thornwatch Halberd plants a line of thorns, the Cinder Crown sends a wave of flame, and the Accord Longsword's skill is now an overhead leap-strike that rallies nearby allies.
- Epic Fight combat with dodges, stamina and movesets. Champions (elite mobs) and mob levelling.
- Origins with **Heritage** and **Fate** layers, and the **Shard-Born** passive skill tree.
- The karambits use the Karambit Vault models and first-person animations. Vanilla swords keep their normal look. Both resource packs can be switched off in Options → Resource Packs.

### Just for fun
- **Target dummies** that show the damage of every hit and can wear armour (MmmMmmMmmMmm), good for trying out weapons.
- **Bicycles** you can craft and ride (Real Bicycles).
- **Minigames to play alone or with friends:** arcade cabinets (Tetris, Pac-Man, Pong, Snake, Space Invaders, Donkey Kong), chess and board games, playable instruments and MIDI songs, film cameras whose photos you can hang up, paintable canvases, and soccer.

### Quality of life
- The land around spawn is pre-generated in the background on a new world's first load, so exploring stutters less, and a chat line says when it is done. `/chunky pause` stops it.
- **Controlling** adds a search bar to Options → Controls, so you can find and fix keybinds among the pack's many mods (and see conflicts).
- Smoother play: structure searches run in the background, structures generate faster, XP orbs merge, mobs that picked up items still despawn, faster crafting lookups, memory-leak fixes, and the game idles when its window is in the background.
- Lighter video and sound-physics settings on first launch (render distance 10). Colonies are capped at 150 citizens and raids at 40 raiders, to keep frame rates steady. Your own settings are never overwritten.
- Pack updates never reset your own game settings.

### Install note
- Prefer the **ModSync / Modrinth** instance so Modrinth jars download at launch. **Gods and Heroes was dropped** (2026-10-31): character power is **Origins Heritage + Fate** only; Palladium **FSang18** and **Satsu** remain. See [README.md](README.md).
