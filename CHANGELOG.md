# Changelog

Player-facing changes for each release of Terrestrial Tribulations. The release zip's version comes from `pack_version` in `modpack/curseforge-mods.json`; bump both together.

## 0.9.0 (first public beta, not released yet)

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.18, 143 mods. Recommended memory: 8 GB.

### Story
- **The main campaign, Chapters 1–12**, in the quest book: from the Hollow fields to founding Emberhold, the Shattered Realms, the Iron Choir and the fight for the Crown. It has three endings.
- **Nine story NPCs** with their own skins and branching conversations: Elder Maera, Doran, Captain Rhosk, Pell, Tamsin, Ilsabet, the Stranger, Wren and Archivist Sol. Later chapters' talks unlock as you progress, and finishing a talk ticks off its quest.
- **Shops** run by Doran, Pell, Ilsabet and the Stranger, priced for the chapter you meet them in.
- **14 existing bosses take story roles.** Each has a story name on its boss bar and speaks lines when you fight it.
- Story gates: the realm dimensions (Aether, Twilight Forest, Undergarden, Blue Skies, Otherside) open in Chapter 7. Hero suits and powers arrive with their chapters.

### Side Paths (optional quest tabs)
- **Cogwright Workshop** (Create), **The Deep Engines** (Mekanism and AE2), **Lumen Conclave Studies** (Iron's Spells, Ars Nouveau, Apotheosis), **Emberhold Builders** (MineColonies) and **Wayfarer's Guide** (exploring and tools). Each tab opens when the story introduces its faction.

### Combat and character
- **23 custom weapons**, each with its own 3D model, colours, swing trail and ability. They include karambits, longswords, a halberd, gauntlets, bows, a staff and more.
- Epic Fight combat with dodges, stamina and movesets. Champions (elite mobs) and mob levelling.
- Origins with **Heritage** and **Fate** layers, and the **Shard-Born** passive skill tree.
- The karambits use the Karambit Vault models and first-person animations. Vanilla swords keep their normal look. Both resource packs can be switched off in Options → Resource Packs.

### Quality of life
- The land around spawn is pre-generated in the background on a new world's first load, so exploring stutters less. `/chunky pause` stops it.
- Pack updates never reset your own game settings.
