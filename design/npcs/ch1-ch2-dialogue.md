# Story NPCs: Chapters 1–2 (Easy NPC dialogue scripts)

Set these up in the **Easy NPC** editor (sneak + right-click an NPC with the Easy NPC wand → Dialog). Use *Advanced Dialog* so the player gets reply buttons.
Placement: inside the Emberhold colony (MineColonies) from Chapter 2. During Chapter 1, Maera stands at the refugee camp (a campfire ring with tents).

## Elder Maera: mentor of the Ember Accord
**Look:** an old woman, ash-grey braids, a crimson shawl with brass pins (Ember Accord colours). **Voice:** calm, dry, protective.
**Role:** main quest giver for Ch1–2. Introduces the Shard-Born mystery.

**Dialogue: "First Meeting"** (Ch1, quest *Voices in the Smoke*)
1. **Maera:** You walked out of the Hollow fields. On your own feet.
   - ▶ "I don't remember how I got there." → 2
   - ▶ "Who are you people?" → 3
2. **Maera:** Nobody does, the first time. The Hollowed take memories before they take the rest. But they didn't take *you*. → 4
3. **Maera:** The Ember Accord. Or what's left of it: forty mouths, six spears and a great deal of stubbornness. → 4
4. **Maera:** I watched a pack of them part around you like water around a stone. In sixty years I've never seen that.
   - ▶ "What does that mean?" → 5
   - ▶ "Lucky, I suppose." → 6
5. **Maera:** It means something in you is older than the ash. The old songs called people like that *Shard-Born*. I thought they were stories. → 7
6. **Maera:** Luck doesn't make the dead step aside. Sit. → 7
7. **Maera:** Eat first. Then I'll ask for your help, and you'll be free to say no. Most do. → 8
8. **Maera:** We need a blade-hand who doesn't die easily. Here, an old scout's pattern. Ember karambits. Small enough to hide, cruel enough to matter. *(completes the quest, unlocks "Teeth for the Night")*

**Dialogue: "The Charter"** (Ch1 finale, *The Ember Accord*)
1. **Maera:** You came back. With all your fingers, even.
2. **Maera:** Then here's the hard part. We can't keep running. We need walls, a hearth, a name.
   - ▶ "Where?" → 3
   - ▶ "Why me?" → 4
3. **Maera:** Somewhere with water, stone and wood. You choose. You've seen more of this land than any of us. → 5
4. **Maera:** Because the Hollowed won't touch you, and anyone standing behind you is safer for it. → 5
5. **Maera:** *(hands over the Emberhold Charter)* Plant this where you mean to stay. We'll follow. *(Chapter 2 begins)*

**Ambient lines (random greeting):** "Ash in the wind again." · "Eat something. You look like a ghost." · "The children ask about you. Try not to die; it would upset them."

## Doran: blacksmith
**Look:** broad, soot-blackened, leather apron, ember-orange forearm tattoos. **Voice:** blunt, few words, secretly sentimental.
**Role:** Ch2 weapon smith. Forges the Ashfang Karambit. Later the weapon-upgrade NPC.
**Trades (Easy NPC trading):** iron ingot ×3 → Rustbite Karambit · flint ×8 → bone shard ×4 · emerald ×2 → iron ingot ×3

**Dialogue: "Fangs"** (Ch2, after the outpost defence)
1. **Doran:** That thing that led the pack. You kept its teeth?
   - ▶ "Two of them." → 2
   - ▶ "Why would I?" → 3
2. **Doran:** Good. Hollow bone holds heat like nothing else. → 4
3. **Doran:** Because Hollow bone holds heat like nothing else, and I'm out of good steel. → 4
4. **Doran:** Give me your karambit and the fangs. Come back at dusk.
5. **Doran:** *(at dusk)* Ashfang. Curved like the dunes. It bites twice. Don't make me regret it. *(rewards Ashfang Karambit)*

**Ambient:** "Hammer's hot. Talk fast." · "Bring me something worth forging." · "Hm."

## Captain Rhosk (Thornwatch): introduced in Ch2, faction lead in Ch5
**Look:** tall, green-and-bronze Thornwatch plate, thorn sigil on the cloak. **Voice:** formal, suspicious, honourable.
**Ch2 intro line:** "The Thornwatch remembers Emberhold's old walls. We'll see whether you deserve new ones."
