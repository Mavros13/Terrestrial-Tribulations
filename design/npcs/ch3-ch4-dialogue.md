# Story NPCs: Chapters 3–4 (Easy NPC dialogue scripts)

Same setup as `ch1-ch2-dialogue.md`: Easy NPC editor (sneak + right-click with the Easy NPC wand → Dialog), *Advanced Dialog* for reply buttons. Quest names in *italics* are working titles; the quest book isn't expanded until the mod list is final, so they get wired up then.
Story beats from `docs/00-kickoff.md`: **Ch3 Echoes of the Old World** (ruins, Old Engine fragments, Create is introduced; climax: Ruined Foundry guardian). **Ch4 The Awakening** (the first Shard reacts to the player, Fate selection unlocks; climax: mirror duel against your Hollow self, which drops the Hollowbane Tachi).

## Pell: Cogwright Guild tinker (Ch3 to Ch5)
**Look:** wiry, goggles pushed up into frizzy copper hair, a coat with more pockets than coat, brass prosthetic left hand that ticks. **Voice:** fast, delighted, forgets to finish sentences.
**Role:** introduces Create in Ch3; gives the Cogwright Breaker line (Ch3–5); later the tech-tier NPC. Placement: a tent full of scrap beside the first ruin, moving into Emberhold once a workshop is built.
**Trades:** andesite alloy ×8 → shaft ×16 · copper ingot ×6 → cogwheel ×8 · Old Engine fragment ×1 → emerald ×4 · copper ingot ×12 → emerald ×1 · emerald ×2 → large cogwheel ×2 · emerald ×3 → water wheel ×1 · emerald ×5 → engineer's goggles ×1 · emerald ×8 → mechanical press ×1

*Shop balance: open from Ch3, when Create starts. Parts that save grinding, priced above crafting cost; the press is the Ch3–4 milestone, so it stays expensive. No brass-tier parts (Ch5+ quests).*

**Dialogue: "The Ticking Hand"** (Ch3 opener, *Echoes of the Old World*)
1. **Pell:** Don't touch that! It's... oh. You're not a Hollow. Good. Sorry. Hello.
   - ▶ "What is this place?" → 2
   - ▶ "Your hand is ticking." → 3
2. **Pell:** A Tinker-King relay station. Seven hundred years old and the gears still mesh. Do you know how rude that is to modern engineering? → 4
3. **Pell:** It's always ticking. Escapement's a bit loose. It keeps time better than I do, honestly. → 4
4. **Pell:** The Old Engines ran on the Convergence. Ley-lines, all of it. When the Crown broke, they stopped. But the *parts* didn't forget how to turn.
   - ▶ "Can you make them work again?" → 5
   - ▶ "Why does that matter to Emberhold?" → 6
5. **Pell:** Not on ley-power. On water. On wind. On stubbornness. Bring me andesite and I'll show you. → 7
6. **Pell:** Walls need stone, stone needs cutting, and nobody in your settlement wants to cut it by hand forever. → 7
7. **Pell:** Here. A waterwheel diagram and a very small amount of my patience. *(rewards the Create starter kit; unlocks the Create tab)*

**Dialogue: "Pressure"** (Ch3, after the Ruined Foundry guardian falls)
1. **Pell:** You *broke* the Foundry guardian? That was a priceless... no, you're right, it was trying to kill you. Fair.
2. **Pell:** Its pressure core survived, though. Look at that. Still warm.
   - ▶ "Can it be used?" → 3
   - ▶ "Is it dangerous?" → 4
3. **Pell:** Everything can be used. → 5
4. **Pell:** Extremely! → 5
5. **Pell:** I'll build it into a hammer head. Every hit builds pressure, and when you let go... *(makes an explosion noise with his mouth)* *(starts the Cogwright Breaker forging quest)*

**Ambient:** "Don't lick the copper." · "If it turns, it's useful." · "Have you seen a 3-millimetre spring? About so big? Never mind."

## Tamsin Rootwarden: Verdant Pact warden (Ch3, returns in Ch7)
**Look:** bark-brown skin painted with green spirals, a cloak of living moss, a longbow strung with root fibre. **Voice:** quiet, unhurried, speaks of the forest as "we".
**Role:** Verdant Pact contact; gives the Rootstring Bow (Ch3); points toward the Twilight Shard in Ch7. Placement: an old oak on the edge of the nearest forest biome; never enters the colony.

**Dialogue: "The Root Remembers"** (Ch3 side line, *The Quiet Wood*)
1. **Tamsin:** You walk loudly for a Shard-Born.
   - ▶ "You know what I am?" → 2
   - ▶ "Sorry. Who are you?" → 3
2. **Tamsin:** The trees noticed before we did. Roots feel the Convergence the way you feel a storm in your knees. → 4
3. **Tamsin:** A warden of the Verdant Pact. We keep the old woods from forgetting themselves. → 4
4. **Tamsin:** Your little engine-man is digging up the Tinker-Kings' bones. We don't forbid it. We ask that you put back what you take.
   - ▶ "I can do that." → 5
   - ▶ "Or what?" → 6
5. **Tamsin:** Then plant ten saplings where you cut, and come back. → 7
6. **Tamsin:** Or the forest takes it back itself, more slowly and much less politely. Ten saplings, please. → 7
7. **Tamsin:** *(on return)* Good. The roots will remember you kindly. This bow was grown, not made. Its arrows root what they strike. *(rewards Rootstring Bow)*

**Ambient:** "Shh. Listen." · "We are older than your walls, and we will be here after." · "The wind says the Hollowed are moving west."

## Elder Maera: the Awakening (Ch4)

**Dialogue: "The Shard Wakes"** (Ch4 opener, *The Awakening*; plays after the player first picks up a Crown Shard)
1. **Maera:** Your hands are shaking. Sit down before you fall down.
2. **Maera:** You heard it, didn't you. When you touched that stone.
   - ▶ "A voice. It knew my name." → 3
   - ▶ "I didn't hear anything." → 4
3. **Maera:** Then it's worse than I feared, and better than I hoped. → 5
4. **Maera:** You're a terrible liar. Good. Honest people usually are. → 5
5. **Maera:** That's a Crown Shard. Queen Ysolde broke the Crown into seven. And you, child, are made of the same stuff.
   - ▶ "That's impossible." → 6
   - ▶ "What do I do?" → 7
6. **Maera:** So is walking through a Hollow pack unharmed. You've had a strange month. → 7
7. **Maera:** You choose what to become. The Shard will make you stronger, whichever path you take. Choose before it chooses for you. *(opens the Fate choice: Origins' Fate layer)*

**Dialogue: "The Voice"** (Ch4, after Fate selection; Maera reacts to what the player heard)
1. **Maera:** Did it say anything else?
   - ▶ "It said it was saving the world." → 2
   - ▶ "It called itself the Regent." → 3
2. **Maera:** They always say that. Every tyrant in every song. → 4
3. **Maera:** Malric Thorne. Ysolde's chancellor. He died at the Sundering, or so the songs say. → 4
4. **Maera:** Whatever he is now, he's in the Shards. Every one you gather brings him closer. Every one you leave lets someone worse find it. There's no clean road here.
5. **Maera:** Go to the old shrine on the ridge. If the Shard showed you something, it'll show you more there. Take your best blade. *(starts the mirror-duel quest)*

**Dialogue: "Your Own Face"** (Ch4 finale, after the Hollow self falls)
1. **Maera:** You came back. Was it...
   - ▶ "It was me. A hollow me." → 2
   - ▶ "I don't want to talk about it." → 3
2. **Maera:** Then you've seen what the Regent wants you to become. Now you know its face. That's worth more than the blade it dropped. → 4
3. **Maera:** Then don't. But keep the blade. Whatever it was, it fought with your hands. → 4
4. **Maera:** Hollowbane, the old smiths called that steel. It cuts what's left of a soul. Use it on the things that deserve it. *(Hollowbane Tachi lore unlock; Chapter 5 begins)*

**Ch4 ambient (Maera, after the Awakening):** "You're quieter lately. That's not a complaint." · "If the voice talks to you at night, wake me." · "Eat. Shard-Born or not, you're still mostly stomach."
