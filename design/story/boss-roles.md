# Boss roles in the campaign (existing mods only)

Every major boss comes from an existing mod and gets a **story role**, a chapter, a lore name in the quest text, and a drop that feeds our weapons or the hero gates. We don't change their models or fights. Difficulty can be tuned later through each mod's config and Champions.

Notes:
- Boss names in tooltips and quests use the lore names (for example, "The Choir Herald (Harbinger)"). The mods' own names stay visible in JEI so players aren't confused.
- Entity IDs for kill tasks and Passive Skill Tree keystone gates are confirmed from `/kubejs dump_registry minecraft:entity_type` after Test Build 2.
