# Ashfang Karambit
- **ID:** `tt:ashfang_karambit`
- **Family / Epic Fight category:** Karambit / `dagger` moveset (dual-wield allowed)
- **Tier / rarity:** Tier 3 (Adventurer) / Uncommon
- **Base stats:** 5 attack damage, 2.2 attack speed, 600 durability, -0.5 reach
- **Innate skill: "Ember Hook"** (Epic Fight weapon innate): a hooking slash that pulls you behind the target, the next hit within 2 s is a guaranteed crit, 12 s cooldown
- **Passive:** hits on burning enemies deal +15% damage
- **Story role:** Ch2 "The First Settlement". Doran the blacksmith forges it from a Hollowed fang after the outpost defence. Lore: "Curved like the ash dunes. Bites twice."
- **Acquisition:** quest reward (Ch2 finale) + craftable later: 2 Hollow Fang + 1 iron ingot + 1 blaze powder at Doran's anvil (KubeJS recipe), visible only after quest `ch2_outpost_defended`
- **Fate synergy:** Duelist (crit scaling), Reaper (burn into lifesteal)
- **Blockbench model:** `blockbench/models/ashfang_karambit.bbmodel`, 600 polygons or fewer, 32×32 texture
