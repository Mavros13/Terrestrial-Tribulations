# Placing the story NPCs

The nine story NPCs are ready-made **Easy NPC presets** in the pack (`kubejs/data/tt/easy_npc/preset/`, generated by
`tools/gen_npcs.py` from the scripts in `design/npcs/`). Each one already has its name, colour, skin and every
conversation from the scripts. Right-click an NPC to get a menu with one button per conversation (labelled by chapter)
plus **Chat**, which shows its ambient lines.

## How players meet them (NPC sites, 2026-10-29)
Each NPC lives at its own small structure, generated by `tools/gen_npc_sites.py` (structures, worldgen, maps and
`kubejs/config/tt_npc_sites.json`) and run by `kubejs/server_scripts/tt_npc_sites.js`:

| Site | NPCs | Opens with (quest that gives the map) | Where it generates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refugee camp | Maera, Doran | always (placed 24 blocks from world spawn on first load) | spawn |
| Thornwatch outpost | Rhosk | c2_found | plains/savanna/taiga-type biomes |
| Pell's ruin | Pell | c2_stage2 | same |
| Tamsin's grove | Tamsin | c3_pell | forests |
| Conclave tower | Ilsabet | c4_mirror | plains-type |
| Stranger's caravan | The Stranger | c5_town | plains-type |
| Wren's lookout | Wren | c6_nether | snowy biomes |
| Sol's archive | Sol | c6_ready | plains-type |

- Until its quest is done (stage `tt_meet_<npc>`), walking into a site puts you back outside with a line. Creative and
  spectator bypass.
- The quest also hands out an explorer map to the nearest site (vanilla `exploration_map`; Async Locator keeps the
  search off the main thread).
- The first time a player who may enter steps inside, the NPC appears on its marker block, **once per world**; other
  copies of the site stay empty ruins.
- `/place structure tt:npc/<site>` places one for testing; `/kubejs custom_command tt_sites` places all eight in a row.
  Untested in game.

## Moving into Emberhold (2026-10-29, owner's choice: the player invites them)
From the chapter below, the NPC's menu shows **Come to Emberhold** (`MOVES` in `tools/gen_npcs.py`). Pressing it moves
that same NPC (never a copy) next to the first **built** MineColonies building of its type in the player's colony, or
next to the Town Hall if there is none yet. The button disappears afterwards. Their site stays behind, empty.

Inside the building they stand beside a block that fits them, facing it. The script searches the building's walls
for the first block on their list. If none is there (MineColonies styles differ), they stand at the building's hut block.

| NPC | Invite opens with | Moves to | Stands at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elder Maera | `tt_emberhold` (Plant the Charter) | Town Hall | the Town Hall block, or a lectern |
| Doran | `tt_emberhold` | Blacksmith's Hut | an anvil, then smithing table, blast furnace, furnace |
| Captain Rhosk | `tt_ch2_done` | Guard Tower, Barracks Tower or Barracks | a target, a grindstone, a banner |
| Pell | `tt_ch3_done` | Mechanic's Hut or Sawmill | a crafting table, smithing table, stonecutter, anvil |
| Ilsabet | `tt_ch5_done` | Enchanter's Tower or Library | an enchanting table, brewing stand, lectern |
| Wren | `tt_ch6_done` | Town Hall | a door or fence gate (the way in) |
| Archivist Sol | `tt_ch7_done` | Library | a lectern, then a bookshelf |

Every free tile beside those blocks is remembered, and **each time the NPC's chunk loads they start on a random one**:
Doran is at the anvil one day and the blast furnace the next, Maera on a different side of the Town Hall block.

Tamsin never comes into the colony, and the Stranger keeps his caravan.

## Looking busy (2026-10-29)
Easy NPC 1.20.1 has poses and its own limb and head motion, but no keyframe animations for its NPCs (checked against
its wiki and changelogs; animation libraries like playerAnimator only drive players). So each NPC holds a tool of
their trade (`WORK` in `tools/gen_npcs.py`) and, while a player is within 20 blocks, works every few seconds: an arm
swing plus a sound and a few particles at their hands. Doran hammers with sparks at the anvil, Rhosk drills sword
swings, Ilsabet chimes amethyst, Sol turns pages, Tamsin tends a sapling. They still turn to look at you. Inviting someone before you have a Town Hall
gets a line telling you to plant the charter first.

## Placing one by hand (cheats on, stand where it should go)

```
/easy_npc preset import_new data tt:easy_npc/preset/humanoid_slim/maera.npc.snbt ~ ~ ~
```

| NPC | Preset | Where the scripts put them |
|---|---|---|
| Elder Maera | `humanoid_slim/maera` | Refugee camp in Ch1, then the Emberhold Town Hall |
| Doran | `humanoid/doran` | Emberhold forge (Blacksmith hut) |
| Captain Rhosk | `humanoid/rhosk` | Emberhold walls / guard tower |
| Pell | `humanoid/pell` | Scrap tent at the first ruin, later a Create workshop in Emberhold |
| Tamsin Rootwarden | `humanoid_slim/tamsin` | Old oak at the edge of the nearest forest (never inside the colony) |
| Ilsabet | `humanoid_slim/ilsabet` | Conclave tower in Emberhold (Town stage) |
| The Stranger | `humanoid/stranger` | Emberhold market from Ch6 (remove after Ch8) |
| Wren | `humanoid_slim/wren` | Emberhold gate |
| Archivist Sol | `humanoid/sol` | Emberhold library (Town stage) |

Tab completion after `data` lists all of them. In the Easy NPC editor (sneak + right-click with the wand) you can then
change the skin, pose or position; conversations live under *Dialog*.

## Notes
- Talking never gives items: rewards stay in the quest book. The quests that say "Talk to …" are still ticked by hand.
- To change a line, edit `design/npcs/*.md` and rebuild; placed NPCs keep their old dialog until re-imported.
- Menus follow the story: a chapter's conversations appear once the previous chapter's quest stage is set (`tt_ch<N-1>_done`; Ch1 and Ch2 are always open). For testing, `/kubejs custom_command tt_story` unlocks them all.
- Each NPC wears its own skin (`tools/gen_npc_skins.py`, painted from the **Look** lines; review sheet `blockbench/previews/npc-skins.png`).
- A dialog shows at most six buttons, so hubs page with "More..." (2026-10-29 test: Doran's seven buttons piled up in one spot).
- Shops: Doran, Pell, Ilsabet and the Stranger have a **Trade** button with the trades from their scripts (`**Trades:**` lines, item names mapped in `gen_npcs.py` `TRADE_ITEMS`). It appears with their first conversation; offers restock every 20 minutes. The self-test checks every traded item exists (`npc_trade_items`).
